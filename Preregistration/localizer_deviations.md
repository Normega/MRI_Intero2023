| 2026-11-28 | **Circle-tracking press check: the logged measure is invalid. The mechanism was misread, and the gate result is withdrawn.** The run's own QC counts showed the problem. 974 of 1,085 stored presses (in runs with a clean trigger grid) came *after* the computed trigger time, which the "first key wins" assumption forbids. A diagnostic (no new score) settles the mechanism: **the log stores the LAST key in the window.**
- 3,563 of 3,570 stored `5`s fall on the last trigger in the window. That includes the 30 windows containing two triggers, where the second one is stored.
- 989 of 1,085 stored presses come after the last trigger. The rest mostly sit at the tolerance edge or have the trigger outside the window.

**Consequences.**
- A press is visible only if it falls after the window's trigger. Presses before it, including presses roughly synchronous with the phase change, are overwritten.
- The data are therefore left-censored, not right-censored. F1 and RMST1.5 as defined measure the wrong quantity.
- **Values seen before the error was found:**
  - Extero F1 ICC(3,1) .47 [.19, .68] (N 44); Match .25; pooled .57.
  - Spearman ρ with breath-tracking PLV: .09 (Extero), .25 (Match).
  - These are not interpretable, and **the gate is not applied**. The output tables were deleted, not committed.
- Under last-key-wins, only the probability of a press later than t after phase onset is identifiable. It cannot separate a missed press from an early (anticipatory) one, so it is not a tracking-accuracy score.

**Correction to the earlier correction.** The registration's "accuracy cannot be scored" stands. The 2026-11-28 row above is right that real presses are stored on about 22% of trials, but wrong about why.
