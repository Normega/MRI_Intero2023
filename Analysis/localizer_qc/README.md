# Localizer step 1: behaviour, physiology and design QC (pre-registration)

Written 2026-11-27, before the localizer plan (`Preregistration/localizer_plan_draft.md`, v0.2) is locked.
Everything here is regenerated by `Scripts/intero2023_localizer_qc.py` (stages `events motion hpf compliance physio`).

**Blindness.** No localizer BOLD voxel was read. From localizer NIfTIs only the header was used (`shape[3]`, TR); `get_fdata()` is never called. From fMRIPrep only `framewise_displacement` was read from the confounds TSVs. No localizer behavioural or physiological measure was related to MAIA, to breathing-run behaviour, to demographics or to any brain measure. Breath-tracking accuracy is summarised only by its distribution, missingness and run-1 vs run-2 reliability.

**Inputs.**
- Task logs: `sourcedata/logs`.
- Biopac `.acq` files: copied read-only from the shared drive to `sourcedata/physio` (2.6 GB, 67 files).
- BIDS headers and fMRIPrep confounds.
- Existing belt and trigger grades: `Data/resp_calibration.csv` and `Data/physio_coverage.csv`.
- Intermediates are written to `derivatives/localizer_qc_cache/`, outside the repo.

## 1. Events (`Data/events/<sub>_task-localizer_run-<k>_events.tsv`, `events_qc.csv`)
**Coverage.** 105 scanned runs from 54 participants (8 on v1, 46 on v2). Every run has a log attempt of record.
- Attempt of record: the attempt with the most completed blocks.
- For three runs other attempts also exist (sub-070 r1, sub-114 r1, sub-118 r1). In each case the other attempts are aborted, with 2–8 blocks.
- **Log-to-scan identity.** The stimulus PC clock runs 117–208 s behind the scanner clock, so absolute agreement cannot be used. Instead, each run's offset must agree with the participant's other localizer run: 102 runs agree within 7.3 s (median 0.14 s), and 3 single-run participants cannot be checked.

**Timing.** Taken from `InteroPilot.4.51.py` and checked on every block.
- **v2.** Task onset is the block's first `Trial` row (Fixation + 2.0 s). The exception is Rest, whose onset is Fixation + 2.0 s because its Trial row is written at the end of the block (row − 29.5 s gives the same onset to within 0.1 s).
- **v1 (correction to the brief).** The onset offset is not the same for every block.
  - The circle tasks (Extero, Match, Intero) start 8.21–8.28 s after the instruction row.
  - **Self and Rest start 6.19–6.25 s after it: v1 had no fixation before Self and Rest.**
  - How each v1 onset is derived:
    - Extero/Match: the first half-cycle row minus 0.8 × the half-cycle. The pacer writes a row at the end of each grow or shrink phase, and the grow phase is 0.8 × the half-cycle.
    - Intero: instruction + the run's Extero/Match offset. This agrees with Completion − 30 s to within 0.04 s.
    - Self: the first word row − 4.5 s.
    - Rest: the Trial row − 29.5 s.
- **Instruction events** (instruction plus fixation) last 8.16–8.29 s. The exception is v1 Self and Rest, at 6.19–6.25 s.

**Task durations.**
- Intero, Self and Rest last 29.99–30.17 s.
- Extero and Match run whole pacer cycles: 30.0–30.35 s in v2, and **30.1–34.4 s in v1**. The v1 pacer half-cycle is 1.88–4.30 s (7–16 /min).

**10-block check.** 103 runs have 10 blocks (2 per condition).
- **sub-058 run-2 and sub-114 run-2: the logs stop at 160 s and 153 s, but the scans are full length (190 and 196 volumes).** The task program stopped during the 5th block's instruction or fixation, and the scanner ran on for about 220 s with no task.
  - Only 4 of the 5 conditions occur in each (sub-058 has no Rest; sub-114 has no Intero).
  - The partial events files are written but are flagged `exclude_partial_log`.
  - **Recommend excluding both runs.** sub-058 run-2 also fails the motion gate. sub-114 keeps run-1.
- **6 v1 runs end before the task does** and should be clipped to the scan end, as the breathing-run designs are:
  - sub-053 r1 loses 27.5 s and r2 loses 23.5 s (174 and 176 volumes);
  - sub-058 r1 loses 12.1 s;
  - sub-052 r2 loses 5.8 s;
  - sub-056 r1 and r2 lose 4.1 s each.

## 2. Motion gate (`motion_gate.csv`)
The registered rule (prereg §4): a run is dropped if mean FD > 0.5 mm or more than 25% of its volumes have FD > 0.5 mm.
- **2 of 105 runs fail:** sub-058 r2 (mean FD 0.495, 34% of volumes) and sub-108 r1 (28%). The median mean FD is 0.18 mm.
- **After the gate and the partial-log exclusions: 102 runs, 54 participants (48 with two runs, 6 with one).**

## 3. High-pass check (design only; `hpf_efficiency.csv`, `hpf_efficiency_participant.csv`, `hpf_sweep.csv`, `hpf_vif.csv`)
**Design.** Five task boxcars plus the instruction regressor, SPM HRF, frame times TR·(i + 0.5), clipped to the scan. Efficiency is 1/(c(XᵀX)⁻¹cᵀ). Results below are for the 102 usable runs.

| Contrast | Loss at 128 s, median (range) | Loss at 200 s, median (range) |
|---|---|---|
| C1 Intero−Extero | 51% (18–72) | 22% (1–63) |
| C2 Intero−Self | 49% (13–76) | 24% (1–73) |
| C4 Match−Extero | 51% (8–73) | 26% (1–62) |
| C5 Match−Intero | 55% (8–73) | 27% (2–63) |
| C6 Self−Rest | 54% (8–74) | 25% (2–64) |

- **Joint two-run model** (efficiency summed over runs): 49–54% loss at 128 s and 21–28% at 200 s.
- **Sweep** (median loss across contrasts): 160 s 33–38%; 256 s 18–26%; 320 s 8–11%; 400 s 1–2%; polynomial order 2 9–12%.
- With the derivative HRF, losses are 3–8 points higher.
- **The plan's trigger fires** (the 128 s loss is far above 20%), so the cutoff moves to 200 s. Even at 200 s the median loss is still 22–27%, and more than 20% in 56–68% of runs.

**VIF.**
- With Rest modelled explicitly, VIF is 9–12 for every regressor (no drift: about 9; 128 s: about 12). The reason is that the six regressors tile the run, so together they are nearly collinear with the intercept.
- With Rest as the implicit baseline, VIF is 1.6 (no drift), 1.9–2.1 (200 s) and 2.4–3.3 (128 s).
- The efficiency of the differential contrasts changes by at most 1% (C6 gains 7%).

## 5. Belt usability (`belt_usability.csv`)
**Belt grade of record** (`belt_grade()` applied to `resp_calibration.csv`), across 105 runs: good 62, degraded 15, fail 20, no recording 8.
- No recording: sub-057 and sub-081 have no `.acq`; sub-075 has no BOLD trigger block; sub-052 r2 and sub-053 r2 have no record.

**Trigger timing**, re-derived from the raw `.acq` with the R logic (native-rate TTL edges, 5 s gap segmentation, header wallclock, `grade_time_match`): exact 73, good 16, degraded 2, fail 8, no acq/no block 6.
- This matches `physio_coverage.csv` except for 3 runs that sit on the 2 s exact/good boundary.
- In all 16 "good" runs the pulse count equals the number of volumes; only the header-clock offset differs (2.0–14.3 s). The first pulse is therefore the first volume.

**Belt polarity (new).** The Match-block belt–pacer correlation (participants were instructed to inhale while the circle grows) is −0.32 to −0.91 for every session up to sub-068 and +0.47 to +0.92 from sub-069 on (except sub-071, unresolved).
- There was a hardware or wiring sign change between two consecutive session dates in early 2024: every session before it is negative and every session after it is positive. Within a participant, the sign never disagrees between runs.
- The participant's polarity is set from their Match blocks: 29 participants +1, 11 −1, 1 unresolved (sub-071, |r| = .17).
- **Independent confirmation:** Intero presses sit at the same belt phase in both polarity groups (−64° vs −65°).
- `R/intero_resp.R` calls polarity unresolvable. It is resolvable, but it is session-specific. Any phase-timing measure (I:E ratio, inhale- or exhale-locked analyses) must use this per-participant sign. Rate, RVT and amplitude are unaffected.

**Usable N.**
- **(a) Rate and depth by condition:** belt good or degraded, timing exact or good, complete log: **71 runs, 41 participants** (v1: 6 runs, 4 participants).
- **(b) Breath-level timing** (also needs pulses = volumes and a resolved polarity): **70 runs, 40 participants** (63 runs if "exact" only). For tracking accuracy (compliant blocks with ≥4 belt breaths): **67 runs, 38 participants; 29 with both runs.**

## 6. Breathing by condition (`breathing_by_condition.csv`)
**Method.**
- Belt band-pass 0.05–1 Hz. Breaths run trough → peak → trough, each of 1.5–20 s.
- Block windows are [onset + 3 s, offset]. Rate = 60 / median period. Depth = median breath amplitude divided by the run's median amplitude.
- RVT is the TR-level `rvt_z` from `Data/regressors`.
- Results are participant means over runs (all = v1 + v2, N = 41).

| | Rest | Self | Intero | Extero | Match |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rate, brpm (median [IQR]) | 13.7 [3.9] | 13.4 [5.5] | 13.4 [3.6] | 12.2 [1.5] | 12.6 [2.0] |
| Relative depth | 0.75 | 0.80 | 1.40 | 1.04 | 1.80 |
| RVT z | −0.42 | −0.35 | 0.53 | −0.20 | 0.74 |
| PLV with pacer (virtual for Rest, Self and Intero) | 0.28 | 0.25 | 0.30 | 0.57 | 0.87 |

**Intero vs Extero** (paired, all participants):
- Rate does not differ: +0.39 brpm, dz .31, p = .13.
- **Intero breathing is deeper:** relative depth dz .59, p < .001; **RVT dz .88, p < .001.** The v2-only results are the same.

**Entrainment** (v2, 37 participants). Unintended entrainment in Extero is substantial.
- **Extero:**
  - PLV 0.57 vs a same-participant virtual-pacer null of 0.32 (p < .001; 32 of 37 above the null);
  - rate / pacer ratio 1.02, with 22 of 33 within 10% of the pacer;
  - the rate IQR shrinks to 1.0 brpm, against 3.6 at Rest.
- **Match:** PLV 0.87 (36 of 37 above the null); 25 of 37 within 10% of the pacer.
- **v1 (4 participants):** Extero PLV 0.51 vs 0.31, and Match 0.71. The v1 data are too few to separate from v2.

## 7. Breath-tracking accuracy (`tracking_accuracy_runs.csv`, `tracking_accuracy_reliability.csv`)
**Pre-specified rule** (fixed in the script before any accuracy value was computed):
- In compliant Intero blocks, a '1' press matches a belt inhale onset (trough) and a '2' press matches an exhale onset (peak) if press − onset ∈ [−1.0, +2.0] s.
- Matching is greedy, earliest press first, with each press used at most once.
- A breath is correct when both onsets are matched and the 1 comes before the 2.
- The lag is press − onset over matched presses. A false press is a press in the block that is matched to nothing.
- Chance is estimated from 200 circular shifts of the presses within each block.

**Results with the pre-specified rule: at chance.**
- Proportion correct, median .37 (IQR .23–.59) vs chance .38; with the polarity flipped, .36.
- Lag median −0.47 s (window-truncated); SD 0.48 s.
- False presses: proportion .31.

**Why it fails.** Presses are tightly locked to the breath, but they lead the belt extremum by a constant amount: phase −66°, about **0.85 s** (IQR 0.71–1.06 s). The window was centred on the wrong place.

**Window-free and post-hoc measures.**
- **Press–breath phase-locking** (resultant length of belt phase at the presses; independent of polarity): median **.84** (IQR .76–.88) vs chance .49; 66 of 67 runs above chance.
- **Post hoc, labelled as such:** the same pair rule with a ±1.5 s window centred on the lead estimated from the *other* Intero block of the run (cross-validated). Median **.83** (IQR .60–.92) vs chance .37; 62 of 62 runs above chance.

**Reliability, run 1 vs run 2:**

| Measure | n | ICC(3,1) | r |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-specified proportion correct | 29 | .41 | .41 |
| Cross-validated proportion correct | 26 | .56 | .57 |
| Phase-locking | 29 | .43 | .44 |
| Lag SD | 29 | .53 | .54 |
| Lead | 26 | .36 | .37 |
| False-press proportion | 29 | .41 | .42 |

**Missingness.** Of 103 runs with complete logs (54 participants), 67 (38 participants) are measured:
- 27 lost to a failed or absent belt;
- 5 to timing;
- 1 to polarity;
- 3 with no compliant block or fewer than 4 belt breaths.

## Recommended plan changes (for the PI to merge)
1. **High-pass.** The registered trigger fires: register a **200 s** cosine set for the localizer.
   - **Drop fMRIPrep's `cosine_*` columns,** which are 128 s, from the "same nuisance set", or the 128 s filter comes back in.
   - State that 200 s still costs about 25% of contrast efficiency (median). Add 128 s and 320 s as sensitivity analyses. If the PI prefers efficiency to drift control, 320 s (8–11% loss) is the alternative.
2. **Model Rest as the implicit baseline.** VIF falls from about 10 to about 2, and the differential contrasts are unchanged. C6 becomes Self vs baseline, and "each condition − Rest" becomes each condition's beta.
3. **Events.**
   - Fix §1's "6 s instruction + 2 s fixation" for v1. In v1, Self and Rest have no fixation, and Extero and Match last up to 34.4 s.
   - Use the event files' exact durations, clipped to the scan end (6 v1 runs).
   - **Exclude sub-058 run-2 and sub-114 run-2** (the task stopped mid-run).
4. **Exclusions and N.**
   - Motion gate: 2 runs fail, leaving 102 runs from 54 participants.
   - Intero contrasts: ≥3 of 4 compliant, N = 44. For group maps, state the one-run rule: 2 of 2 compliant gives N = 48.
   - fROI: N = 44. **Transfer (fROI ∩ H3): N = 40; minimum detectable dz .45, not .43.**
5. **Breathing covariate for C1.** Extero entrains, and Intero breathing is deeper (RVT dz .88).
   - C1 therefore carries a large respiratory difference, and the +RVT⊗RRF model should no longer be an optional sensitivity analysis.
   - Making it primary is costly: RVT regressors exist for 75 of the 102 usable runs (44 participants), and only 29 of the 44 fROI-eligible participants have them on both runs.
   - **Recommendation:** keep the no-physio model as primary at full N. Register the +RVT model as a **required co-report for L1, with a stated rule:** L1 is described as respiration-robust only if C1 keeps its sign and p < .05 in the RVT subset. Add per-participant ΔRVT (Intero − Extero) and Extero PLV as group-level sensitivity covariates.
   - The fROI rule stays on the primary model.
6. **Breath-tracking accuracy.**
   - Do **not** register the fixed-window pair proportion; it is at chance.
   - If accuracy is registered, register the cross-validated, lead-centred pair proportion (or phase-locking as the window-free alternative). Label it **exploratory**, and disclose that the centring was chosen after the fixed window failed.
   - The basis for that recommendation: N = 38 measured; reliability ICC ≈ .4–.56 with n = 26–29; it is the study's only objective accuracy measure, but its correlations with MAIA, detection or C1 are attenuated by about √.5.
   - Belt polarity must be set per participant, from Match.
   - "False presses" cannot count repeated same-key presses, which are not logged.
7. **Entrainment and adherence.** Register PLV against the same participant's virtual-pacer null, not raw PLV: the null alone is about .32 over 30 s.
